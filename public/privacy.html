Privacy Policy

Academic Reader, operated by Initial Loop · Effective 25 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the Academic Reader web app at academic-reader.initialloop.com ("the app"), including its optional integrations with Dropbox and Google Drive. The app is operated by Initial Loop ("we"). Contact: support@initialloop.com.

2. Summary

  • The app runs in your browser. We do not operate servers that receive, process or store your documents, API keys, files or usage.
  • We have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and we do not sell or share personal information.
  • Data you create in the app is stored in your browser and, if you enable sync, in your own Dropbox or Google Drive.
  • Your browser communicates directly with third-party services (listed in section 5) when you use features that need them.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app is delivered as static files by our hosting provider, Netlify, which may record standard web server logs, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and time, as part of operating its service. We do not use these logs to identify or track users. Netlify's handling of this data is described in the Netlify Privacy Statement.

4. Data stored in your browser

The app stores the following on your device using your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage. It is not transmitted to us.

  • Documents you open or import, and guides, questions, answers and flashcards created for them.
  • Settings, including an optional description of yourself that is sent to Anthropic with requests (section 5).
  • Your Anthropic API key, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, either with a key derived from a passphrase you choose or with a non-extractable key generated by your browser.
  • Access tokens for Dropbox or Google Drive, if you connect them.
  • An error log, with keys removed.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

6. Dropbox

If you connect Dropbox, the app requests access with the permissions files.content.read and files.content.write, limited to an app folder (Apps/Academic Reader). It cannot see or change any other files in your Dropbox.

  • What is stored: files containing your documents, guides, questions and answers, flashcards and reading position, in the app folder.
  • How it is accessed: directly from your browser to Dropbox, using a token held in your browser. We have no copy of the token and no access to your Dropbox.
  • Purpose: solely to keep your library the same across your own devices.
  • Revoking access: disconnect in the app's Settings, or remove the app under Settings → Connected apps in your Dropbox account. Deleting the Apps/Academic Reader folder deletes the synced data.

7. Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which allows it to access only files it has created. It creates a folder named Academic Reader and stores the same files as described for Dropbox.

  • Access happens directly from your browser to Google, using a short-lived token held in your browser. We have no copy of the token and no access to your Google account.
  • Academic Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide sync to you, is not transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train models.
  • Revoke access in the app's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Academic Reader folder deletes the synced data.

8. Cookies

The app does not set cookies. It uses browser storage as described in section 4.

9. Shared pages

The app can export a document and its guide as a standalone web page. Pages you export contain the document text and guide, and optionally your saved questions if you choose to include them. They do not contain your API key. Where you publish an exported page is your decision.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Changes

We will update this page if the app's handling of data changes, and revise the effective date above. The source code, and the history of this policy, are public at github.com/techurbanist/academic-reader.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@initialloop.com.
